# Memos

Part of `hwp-agent-edit`; read after the per-document steps in `SKILL.md`.

Memos (Hancom 메모/주석) can be viewed, added, edited and deleted. Click the toolbar button **메모 목록** to open the memo list: each memo is a group named **메모 N** that shows its anchored text and holds the textbox **메모 N 내용** with the buttons **메모 N 저장** and **메모 N 지우기**; close it with **메모 목록 닫기**. Saving rewrites only that memo's body (each line becomes a memo paragraph) and deleting removes the memo while keeping the anchored text. To add a memo, select the target text with **찾아 바꾸기** and **다음 찾기**, close Find, choose **입력 → 메모 넣기**, fill **메모 내용** and click **넣기**. Use exact name matching (Playwright `exact: true` or a trailing `s` in the selector), because **메모 목록** is also a prefix of **메모 목록 닫기**. A new memo must cover a non-empty range inside one paragraph of body text, a table cell or a text box. Paragraphs with footnotes, endnotes, equations, tables or pictures take memos: a memo that ends at an object stops before it, one that starts at an object begins after it, and one that covers an object keeps it inside. The editor refuses ranges that overlap or touch another field (hyperlink, click-here field or memo) or touch an auto number, and paragraphs inside a multi-paragraph field; a refusal leaves the document unchanged. Memos that span several paragraphs (Hancom allows them) are listed with their anchored text and **(여러 문단)**, and can be edited and deleted. Memo edits go through undo/redo and journal recovery. HWP memo bodies are read from the memo list at the end of the last section, so editing an HWP that carries memos keeps them; engines before this change dropped them. Memo insertion, editing, deletion, undo/redo, recovery and saved reopening pass scoped HWP/HWPX checks, a Hancom-made HWP memo survives an edit and save with its layout, and Hancom itself shows the saved memos with their text and time for body, table-cell, text-box, multi-section and multi-paragraph memos, and for memos next to and over footnotes, an equation and an inline table. Footnotes and endnotes can be inserted through **삽입 → 각주/미주**.
